This page is about Andrew.

O Andrew, scholar of the sacred pitch, Whose cleats and sonnets both are finely stitched, By day thou parse the semicolon’s grace, By eve thou charge across the open space, Where Faulkner’s winding sentence finds its twin In through-balls threaded where the lines begin. And oh, the ball knowledge thou possess! The offside trap, the false nine’s quiet finesse, Half Hemingway, half Haaland in disguise, Thou analyze the game with learned eyes. So here’s to thee, my roommate, bold and wise: May thy goals be many, and thy dishes... one day rise.

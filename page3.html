This page is about James.

O James, whose pom-poms lift the crowd to cheer, Whose splits and stunts defy all gravity’s fear, By day thou fly above the shouting throng, By night thou grind the ranks all evening long, Where every boss falls to thy button-mashing hand, And “just one more round” is thy command. But greater still, O James, thy noblest feat: The pizza, hot and cheesy, thou dost treat! When hunger strikes and my wallet lies bare, Thou answer, generous, with a slice to spare. So here’s to thee, my friend, both kind and spry: Our MVP, our player one, our pizza guy.

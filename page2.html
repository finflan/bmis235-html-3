This page is about Wesley.

O Wesley, monarch of the couch’s embrace, Whose naps are long and slumber leaves no trace, By day thou drain the jumper, smooth and true, By night thou watch the pros do what they do, Then sink into the cushions, deep and low, Where dreams of fast breaks come and softly go. But hark! When hunger stirs thy sleepy head, Thou summon Domino’s with a thumb of dread, The breadsticks come, the pizza box takes flight, A feast delivered to thy throne tonight. So here’s to thee, our hoopster, bold and prone: All-Star of naps, MVP of the phone.
